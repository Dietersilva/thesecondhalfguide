How the estimate was built
Assumptions & calculation notes
  • The default Social Security estimate reconstructs prior covered earnings using an SSA Quick Calculator-style method, applies wage indexing, a 35-year calculation and the applicable benefit formula.
  • An SSA estimate entered under Advanced customization overrides the rough estimate.
  • The portfolio model grows current balances, adds entered contributions while working, and uses Social Security plus pension/other income against the retirement spending target.
  • Taxes, investment volatility, inflation, RMDs, detailed survivor benefits and exact Medicare premiums are not fully modeled yet.

Educational planning tool, not financial, tax or legal advice. Verify important Social Security and Medicare decisions with the issuing agency.
