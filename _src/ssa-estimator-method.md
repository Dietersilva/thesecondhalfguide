# Social Security planning estimator method

The public Retirement Strategy Model does not treat current earnings as average career earnings.

For visitors who do not enter an official SSA estimate, the browser-only calculator uses an SSA Quick Calculator-style approximation:

1. Start with current or most recent annual earnings covered by Social Security.
2. Estimate prior covered earnings using the national Average Wage Index (AWI) series and SSA Quick Calculator's default 2% relative growth assumption.
3. Cap each year's estimated earnings at that year's Social Security contribution and benefit base.
4. Wage-index earnings through age 60.
5. Select the highest 35 indexed years, including zero years when fewer than 35 are available, and compute AIME.
6. Compute the PIA using the bend points for the year the worker reaches 62. Future bend points use the 2026 Trustees Report intermediate AWI assumptions.
7. Apply the statutory early-retirement factor for 62, 65, 67 or another displayed age. Results are presented as planning estimates in today's-dollar terms.

This remains a rough estimate. SSA's personalized estimate based on the actual earnings record should override it whenever the visitor provides one.

Primary references: SSA Quick Calculator FAQ, SSA AWI series, SSA PIA formula, 2026 Trustees Report intermediate AWI assumptions, and SSA contribution/benefit base tables.
