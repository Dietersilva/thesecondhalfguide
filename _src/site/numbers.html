Keep this somewhere

The numbers worth keeping by the phone

Every scam on this site works the same way: someone contacts you, and you deal with them instead of hanging up and calling the real number. This is the real numbers. Print it and put it in a drawer.

Compiled and verified by Edward Silva · checked August 2026 · Print this page for the drawer

Medicare

Medicare

Coverage questions, claims, replacement cards, plan changes, reporting fraud.

Won't recommend a specific plan or sell you anything.

1-800-633-42271-800-MEDICARE · 24 hours
TTY 1-877-486-2048

SHIP — your state's Medicare counselors

Free, trained, one-to-one help comparing plans. They earn nothing whichever you pick.

Find your state's number at shiphelp.org.

shiphelp.orgFree · independent

Senior Medicare Patrol

Help when you suspect Medicare billing fraud or errors on a statement.

Find your state's program at smpresource.org.

smpresource.orgFree · local

Social Security

Social Security Administration

Benefits, survivor claims, earnings record corrections, appointments.

Waits can be long. The website handles most of this faster — see our guide to the new login.

1-800-772-12138am–7pm local, weekdays

Fraud and scams

National Elder Fraud Hotline

Staffed by people trained to help older adults who have been targeted or defrauded. They help you report it to the right places.

Run by the Justice Department's Office for Victims of Crime.

1-833-372-8311833-FRAUD-11
10am–6pm ET, weekdays

FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center

Report online fraud, wire transfers, crypto, gold couriers. Speed matters — fast reports are how money sometimes gets recovered.

Online only; there is no phone line.

ic3.govReport online

Federal Trade Commission

Report any scam, whether or not you lost money. Reports are what get networks shut down.

Report at reportfraud.ftc.gov.

reportfraud.ftc.gov

Local help

Eldercare Locator

The front door to everything local: transport, meals, home care, caregiver support, benefits screening, Adult Protective Services.

Connects you to your Area Agency on Aging — the most under-used resource in this whole subject.

1-800-677-1116eldercare.acl.gov

Benefits screening

Checks what you may qualify for — Medicare Savings Programs, food, energy, property tax relief — in one pass.

See why this is worth twenty minutes.

benefitscheckup.org

Travel

TSA Cares

Arranges a specialist to help you through airport screening. Call at least 72 hours ahead.

Separate from airline wheelchair assistance, which you book with the airline — both are free.

1-855-787-222772 hours ahead

If things are bad

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

Call or text 988. Free, confidential, 24 hours.

For you or for someone you're worried about.

988Call or text · 24 hours

The whole point of this page. If someone contacts you claiming to be any of these organizations, hang up and call the number here instead. Not the number they gave you, and not by pressing 1. That single habit defeats nearly every scam we've written about — see the five-minute rule.

Numbers were checked in August 2026. Government phone lines change rarely, but if one of these is wrong, tell us and we'll fix it the same week.
