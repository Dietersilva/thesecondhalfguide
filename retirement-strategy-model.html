Assumptions & calculation notes
  • The default Social Security estimate uses current annual earnings as a rough proxy for indexed career-average earnings, adjusted for years of covered work, then applies the current PIA formula. Actual SSA benefits use your highest 35 years of wage-indexed earnings.
  • If you enter an SSA estimate under Advanced, that estimate replaces the rough salary-based estimate.
  • The earnings-test section defaults to the 2026 limit and can be changed as rules update.
  • Portfolio growth is a straight-line illustration, not a guarantee or Monte Carlo forecast.
  • Taxes, survivor benefits and staggered spouse claiming can materially change results and should be reviewed separately.

Educational planning tool, not financial, tax or legal advice. Verify Social Security with SSA before making a claiming decision.
