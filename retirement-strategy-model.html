Assumptions & calculation notes

  • Social Security estimates are derived from the monthly benefit amount and claim age you enter, using statutory early-claim reductions and delayed-retirement credits.
  • The 2026 retirement earnings-test input defaults to $24,480 and $1 withheld for every $2 over the limit before full retirement age. Future limits change.
  • Benefits withheld by the earnings test are not treated here as permanently lost; SSA adjusts benefits at full retirement age. The model uses a simplified credit approximation.
  • Portfolio projections use a constant annual return and annual contributions. Real returns are uneven and this is not a Monte Carlo forecast.
  • Tax estimates are intentionally not presented as exact. Social Security taxation, IRMAA, state taxes, Roth/traditional balances and deductions can materially change net results.
  • For couples, this version compares both people using the same claim age in the main table. Survivor and staggered-claim strategies are flagged separately rather than pretending a single combined formula covers every household.

Educational planning tool, not financial, tax or legal advice. Verify Social Security with SSA, Medicare enrollment with Medicare/SSA, tax rules with IRS guidance and employer coverage with the plan administrator before acting.
