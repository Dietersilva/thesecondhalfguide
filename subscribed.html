Wednesday Letters

Check your inbox — there's one more step

We've sent you a short email asking you to confirm. The letter won't start arriving until you click the link inside it.

Why the extra step

Confirming proves the address is really yours. It stops anyone from signing you up for mail you never asked for, and it keeps our list clean enough that the letter lands in inboxes rather than spam folders. It takes one click and you only do it once.

If nothing arrives in a few minutes

  • Look in spam, junk, or Promotions. First emails from a new sender often land there. If you find it, mark it “not spam” — that teaches your mail program to deliver the rest to your inbox.
  • Add us to your contacts. Saving our sending address in your address book is the single most reliable way to keep the letter out of the junk folder.
  • Check the address you typed. A missing letter in the domain is the usual culprit. You can simply sign up again.
  • Still nothing? Email hello@thesecondhalfguide.com and we'll sort it out by hand.

What you've signed up for

One email, Wednesday mornings. A few things that changed this week for people in their late fifties and sixties, with the actual numbers and a link to the full piece. Nothing else — we don't sell, rent, or share your address, and every issue has a one-click unsubscribe link at the bottom. The privacy policy spells it out.

While you wait

Two things worth having already: the numbers worth keeping by the phone, which prints onto a single page for the drawer, and the five-minute rule, which is the one habit that defeats most of the scams we write about.
